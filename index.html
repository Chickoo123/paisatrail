Methodology

How a charge becomes a claim

  1. ReadFinds the header row under bank preambles, Indian date formats, debit/credit or amount + Dr/Cr columns, and the running balance.
  2. IdentifyRecognises charges from their narrations (NON MAINTENANCE OF AMB CHGS, ATM CHGS, NEFT CHGS INB…) and pairs each GST line with the charge it was levied on.
  3. TestApplies a specific RBI rule using only what the statement proves. A finding is Proven when the statement alone establishes it, for example a penalty that took the balance below zero. Anything that depends on outside facts, such as whether the bank sent a notice, is marked Confirm.

What PaisaTrail cannot tell you

  • Your bank's own schedule of charges, or whether you agreed to a fee.
  • Whether a transfer was online, or an ATM was your bank's, unless the narration says so.
  • Charges with narrations it doesn't recognise. Check the "Every charge found" table.
  • Legal advice. Findings are requests to make to your bank.
