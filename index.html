Find bank charges you can get reversed.

Banks deduct minimum-balance penalties, ATM fees, SMS charges and transfer fees, often with GST on top. Some of them conflict with RBI's rules. Upload your savings account statement, and PaisaTrail lists those charges with the rule each one breaks, estimates what you can claim back, and drafts the request.

  • Checks against RBI rules. Each finding links to the circular it relies on.
  • Shows its evidence. Every claim lists the exact statement lines behind it.
  • Your statement stays with you. It's read in your browser, and nothing is uploaded or stored.

How PaisaTrail works

  1. Reads the statement: finds the header row, Indian date formats, debit/credit or amount + Dr/Cr columns, and the running balance.
  2. Finds charges from their narrations (for example NON MAINTENANCE OF AMB CHGS, ATM CHGS, SMS CHGS, NEFT CHGS) and matches each GST line to the charge it was levied on.
  3. Checks each charge against a specific RBI rule, using only what the statement proves. For example, a penalty that takes the balance below zero is provable; whether the bank sent a notice first isn't, so that's marked "Confirm".

What PaisaTrail cannot tell you

  • Your bank's own schedule of charges, or whether you agreed to a particular fee. Ask for the schedule if a charge looks high.
  • Whether a transaction was online or at a branch, or at your own bank's ATM, unless the narration says so.
  • Charges with narrations it doesn't recognise. Check the "Every charge found" table against your statement.
  • Legal advice. Findings are requests to make to your bank, not determinations.
